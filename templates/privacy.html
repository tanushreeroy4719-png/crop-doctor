What we store

Uploaded images are saved to the server's uploads/ folder so they can be displayed back to you and referenced in your history log. Questionnaire answers are sent to the report generator and are not stored beyond what appears in your history entry.

Third parties

If an Anthropic API key is configured, image predictions and your questionnaire answers (not the image itself) are sent to Anthropic's API to generate the written report.

Your choices

Delete files from the uploads/ folder and clear data/history.json at any time to remove stored data.
